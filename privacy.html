Deadpan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Deadpan does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK.

  • Face data (TrueDepth / ARKit): the app uses the front camera and ARKit face tracking to read facial expression values (eye blink, smile and head position) in real time, only to judge the challenge. These values are processed in memory on your device, are never stored, never leave your device, and are not used for identification, authentication, advertising or analytics.
  • Videos: challenge videos are saved inside the app on your device. They leave it only when you choose to share them or save them to Photos.
  • Microphone: used only to add sound to your challenge video. You can turn it off.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Contact: see the support page.

日本語

Deadpanは個人データを収集・送信・共有・販売しません。前面カメラとARKit（TrueDepth）の顔トラッキングで、まばたき・笑顔・頭の位置の値をリアルタイムに読み取り、チャレンジの判定だけに使います。これらの値は端末のメモリ上で処理され、保存も送信もされず、本人確認・広告・解析には使いません。動画は端末内に保存され、ご自身で共有・保存したときだけ端末の外に出ます。マイクは動画に音声を入れるためだけに使います。
